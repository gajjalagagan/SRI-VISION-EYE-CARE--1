Our services

Complete care for your vision

From routine eye checks to specialist assessments, explore common eye-care and surgical service categories. Availability depends on clinical evaluation and the services offered by the centre.

Comprehensive Eye Exams

Vision checks and eye-health assessments for different age groups.

Cataract Care

Assessment, treatment guidance and referral for cataract surgery.

LASIK & Refractive Care

Eligibility assessments and guidance on vision-correction options.

Glaucoma Services

Eye-pressure checks, monitoring and specialist management.

Retina & Diabetic Eye Care

Assessment and referral for retinal and diabetes-related eye concerns.

Optical & Contact Lenses

Help choosing prescription spectacles and suitable contact lenses.

Cornea & Eye Surface

Evaluation of corneal conditions and other eye-surface problems.

Paediatric Eye Care

Child-friendly vision screening and eye-care consultations.

Surgical procedures are performed only by appropriately qualified clinicians at a properly equipped facility, where available.
